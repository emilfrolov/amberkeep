# Community code of conduct

Amberkeep welcomes people with different backgrounds, identities, languages, and levels of experience. These expectations apply in this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces managed by the project.

## Participate with care

- Be respectful and give people room to ask questions and learn.
- Discuss the work with specific, constructive feedback. Accept that others may disagree.
- Respect people's time, boundaries, identities, and privacy.
- Share synthetic examples. Do not publish another person's conversations, account details, or private communications.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, impersonation, and repeated spam are not acceptable. Personal attacks and persistent unwanted contact are not productive ways to resolve a technical disagreement.

## Raise a concern

The project maintainer, [emilfrolov](https://github.com/emilfrolov), handles moderation. For a non-sensitive concern, ask the maintainer to review the relevant thread. For a confidential concern, use a private contact method explicitly provided on the maintainer's profile. If no private method is available, open an issue asking for a private contact without posting identities, evidence, or incident details.

The project currently has no separate moderation team or independent appeal channel. GitHub's own reporting tools remain available for violations of its platform rules, including concerns that cannot safely be raised here.

## Moderation

The maintainer may request changes in behavior, remove inappropriate content, restrict participation, or block accounts. Responses depend on the context, severity, and repeated behavior. Where practical and safe, the maintainer will explain the action and consider additional context privately.

Do not retaliate against someone for raising a concern. Handle reports with discretion and share details only when needed to address the situation.
